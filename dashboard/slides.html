The approach

Five deterministic scoring layers, 0–100

L1 · 0–25RegistrationMetadata quality & completeness.
L2 · 0–25LivenessEndpoint probes: is it reachable?
L3 · 0–25On-chainWallet behavior & transaction history.
L4 · 0–25SybilSpam & clone detection across owners.
L5 · 0–15ReputationExisting on-chain feedback, Sybil-discounted.

Fully deterministic: no LLM, no randomness. Every score is reproducible and auditable, backed by an IPFS-pinned evidence report.

Same inputs → same score, every run.03

Why it can't be gamed

Circuit breakers cap manipulators

  • A mass registrar can't score above 15, no matter how polished its metadata looks.
  • Clone, dead-endpoint, and negative-reputation breakers each hard-cap the score.
  • Weight changes barely move outcomes: rankings hold at Spearman ρ 0.944–0.997 across 5 configs.
78.5%of all 9,400 agents trip at least one circuit breaker.
≤ 15hard ceiling for a detected mass registrar.
Breakers, not weights, decide who is trusted.04
